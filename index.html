Rider's Interactive Fitness Blueprint

Your structured 6-day garage strength rotation + daily 15-minute synced cardio block, designed for posture, lower leg stability, and core self-carriage.

Day 1: Legs & Calves (Circuit A/B/C) Focus: Barbell Squats, Sumo Squats, Split Squats, Calf Raises. Synced cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Mobility Push.
Day 2: Arms & Upper Body Focus: Close-Grip Bench Press, Bicep Curls, Bench Dips, Overhead Tricep Extensions. Synced cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Run block.
Day 3: Core Stability Focus: Weighted Vest Planks, Russian Twists, Hollow Body Holds, Deadbugs. Synced cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Core Push.
Day 4: Glutes & Hamstrings Focus: Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs), Hip Thrusts, Reverse Lunges, Single-Leg Work. Synced cardio: 15-Minute Incline Hill Walk (Self-carriage sync).
Day 5: Shoulders & Back Focus: Standing Overhead Press, Incline Chest Press, DB Rows, Lateral Raises. Synced cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Posture Block.
Day 6: Functional Rider Fitness Focus: Split Squats, Woodchops, Single-Leg Balance, High-Knee Marching. Synced cardio: 11m Walk / 4m Sustained Push.
Day 7: Rest & Active Recovery Focus: 15-20 minute light walk or jog. Complete physical and mental reset.

Remi's Interactive Conditioning Blueprint

All sessions are designed to fit under 15 minutes of high-yield work, paired with your daily rider routine.

Monday: Ground & Pole Work (Setup-Heavy) Raised trot poles and serpentines for core engagement and agility (12-15 mins). Linked rider cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Mobility Push.
Tuesday: Round Pen Canter Intervals Warm up walk/trot 5 mins. Canter 90 secs each lead (3 mins total) with walk breaks. Linked rider cardio: 12m Walk / 3m Jog.
Thursday: Driveway Hill & Quick Transitions (Zero Setup) 4 repeats up paved driveway hill at brisk walk/trot. Linked rider cardio: 15-Minute Incline Hill Walk (Self-carriage sync).
Weekend (Sat or Sun): Mixed Interval / Trot Set Brisk trot sets with short canter extensions. Linked rider cardio: 11m Walk / 4m Sustained Cardio Push.
